Raaqeb sends two kinds of webhook. A [webhook channel](/en/docs/notifications/webhook) receives operator notifications, signed with the Standard Webhooks scheme. A subscriber who subscribes to a status page with a webhook receives subscriber notifications in the body Atlassian Statuspage sends, unsigned.

Every POST carries `webhook-id`, the same on each retry so a receiver can drop a duplicate, and `webhook-timestamp`. A receiver that answers anything but `2xx` gets the POST again 30 seconds, 2 minutes and 10 minutes later.

## Webhook channel

Each notification type is one body, told apart by its `type` field. The channel's page explains [how to verify the signature](/en/docs/notifications/webhook).
